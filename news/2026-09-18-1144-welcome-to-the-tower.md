# Conquest Mode
Conquest is a new mode where players will gain upgrades to their decks and take on an ever-evolving bot. The more you play, the more effects you unlock for the run, which persist between each match. Every time you defeat the bot, the bot's difficulty and capabilities increase. 
As you progress through the Tower, you'll bank cR that will be paid out the moment you lose or retire your run. The higher you get in the tower, the larger your payout becomes with each win. It is the best way to earn cR.
Cards can be upgraded with additional abilities, taking advantage of the system's multi-ability system. How powerful can you get your deck before succumbing to the bot's ever-growing strength?

# Online Debug Mode
A new mode has been added for those who wish to help test cards in the game. Due to how cards must be programmed to specifically work online with host and client communication, there can be a lot more issues that come up. So to combat this, a new only lobby is introduced with built-in debug tools so players can give themselves the cards needed to test. This can only be accessed by activating the Debug menu.

# Updated Bot AI
Bot AI continues to grow, and will continue doing so as time goes on. This update makes sure that lower-difficulty bots makes more and more mistakes. They'll stockpile when they shouldn't, they'll play unoptimal cards, they'll target worse with Events, etc. On top of this, bots are now much more reactive. They'll remember that you have certain cards and will try to bait reactions. They'll also use a lot more cards in much more optimal ways outside of just using cards on their turn. Expect more events to be played when you'd least expect them.

# More fixes
As usual, more and more fixes to cards are being made. This takes time, and hopefully the online debug mode speeds this up greatly.
