# Let's try this again
I made this post before, and in greater detail than this will be. But it didn't post like it should, so I'm trying again.

# Profiles
Profiles are new and here to stay! This is where your information will be found. You'll also find the new friends list and friends/rivals option. This is a serious WIP that is not yet complete, so things will change. There might also be pushes to the server that could briefly interrupt matches at random intervals.

# Friends and Rivals
A new Friends system is being implemented, which is being improved over time. Everyone has a pseudo-profile that is created by their game, that way they don't need to make a profile elsewhere, such as Steam or Epic Online Services. Though if this ever proves to be a problem, we could look into using those services, instead. You can currently add friends, and select a friend to be your rival, in which your stats are compared.

# Optimization
The game has gone through a large optimization sweep, vastly improving the time cards are played, reducing lag overall, and greatly improving the load times of cards and bot actions/reactions to cards. You will feel this the most when a field has dozens of cards, reducing lag anywhere from 4 seconds to 150 milliseconds.
